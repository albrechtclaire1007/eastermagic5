Parent tip

Use real cups or baskets. Put exactly 5 eggs, grapes, coins, or toy pieces in each one. Cover them. Count in 5s only. Do not go back to counting all from 1.

Parent tip

Use real hands or gloves. Ask: “How many hands?” then count 5, 10, 15, 20. Keep the focus on groups, not on counting each finger one by one.

Parent tip

Use empty cups or baskets and let her fill them one by one. Stop at 5. The goal is to feel that one full basket means 5, not to count everything from 1 each time.
